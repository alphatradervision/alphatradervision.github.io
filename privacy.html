Privacy Policy

App: AlphaTrader Studio   Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who uses this app

AlphaTrader Studio is a private tool used only by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channel. No other person signs in to it.

2. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. YouTube's and Google's handling of data is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What data the app accesses

  • The owner's own channel and video information (titles, descriptions, tags, upload status).
  • The owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, audience retention).

The app does not access any data of viewers, commenters or other channels' private data.

4. How the data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos, set their details, and review the channel's own performance to improve future videos.

5. Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer and the owner's own private cloud backup. They are never sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

6. Cookies

The app does not place cookies on any user's device.

7. Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Stored data can be deleted on request by emailing the contact below; it is deleted within 7 days.
