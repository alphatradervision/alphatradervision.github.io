AlphaTrader Studio

AlphaTrader Studio is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner to prepare and upload educational stock-market and IPO videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It is not offered to the public, does not sign in any other users, and does not collect data from viewers.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own videos and Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedule for those videos.
  • Reads the owner's own channel statistics to improve future videos.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.
